To enter your Netflix TV code, launch the **Netflix app** on your TV and note the code shown on the screen. Then visit **Netflix.com/tv2** on another device, sign in to your Netflix account, and enter the code exactly as displayed. After the code is verified, your TV will automatically connect to your Netflix account.

## What You Need

Before you start the netflix.com/tv2 activation process, make sure you have all of the following on hand:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="A Netflix Account" icon="circle-play">
    An active Netflix subscription. You will need your Netflix account email address and password during activation at netflix.com/tv2.
  </Card>

  <Card title="A Supported TV or Streaming Device" icon="tv">
    A Smart TV or streaming device that has the Netflix app installed and is powered on. See the **Supported Devices** section below for a full list of compatible hardware.
  </Card>

  <Card title="A Phone, Tablet, or Computer" icon="mobile">
    A second device — such as a smartphone, tablet, or laptop — with a web browser installed. You will use this device to visit **netflix.com/tv2** and enter your Netflix account credentials.
  </Card>

  <Card title="A Working Internet Connection" icon="wifi">
    Both your TV and your second device must be connected to the internet at the same time. The TV needs connectivity to generate and validate the activation code; your phone or computer needs it to load netflix.com/tv2.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Supported Devices

Netflix TV activation via **netflix.com/tv2** is supported across a wide range of hardware. **Smart TVs** from major manufacturers — including Samsung, LG, Sony, and Vizio — all support Netflix activation through their built-in operating systems (Tizen, webOS, Google TV, and SmartCast respectively). **Streaming sticks and boxes** such as Roku players, Amazon Fire TV sticks and cubes, Apple TV (4th generation and later), and Google Chromecast with Google TV also fully support Netflix activation codes. Finally, **game consoles** including Sony PlayStation (PS4 and PS5) and Microsoft Xbox (One, Series S, and Series X) have the Netflix app that uses the same netflix.com/tv2 activation flow.

## Before You Begin

<Note>
  Make sure **both your TV and your phone or computer are connected to the internet** before you open the Netflix app. Netflix activation codes are time-sensitive — they typically expire after just a few minutes — so have your phone or computer within arm's reach before you launch the app on your TV. If the code expires before you finish, the TV will generate a new one and you will need to start the netflix.com/tv2 process over.
</Note>
