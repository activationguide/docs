Most modern Smart TVs — including Samsung, LG, Sony, and Vizio — support **netflix.com/tv2** activation code sign-in. Instead of typing your Netflix password with the TV remote, you visit **netflix.com/tv2** on your phone or computer and enter the code displayed on your TV.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Samsung">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Smart Hub">
        Press the **Home** button on your Samsung remote to open Smart Hub. This is the central hub for all your apps and content.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Find and launch the streaming app">
        Use the directional pad to navigate to the streaming app you want to sign in to (e.g. Netflix). If it isn't already installed, open the **Apps** section and download it first.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select Sign In">
        Launch the app and select **Sign In** from the welcome screen. You may be prompted to choose a sign-in method — select **Sign in with a web browser** or **Use activation code** if multiple options appear.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Note the URL and code shown">
        The TV will display an activation URL (for example, `netflix.com/tv2`) and a short alphanumeric code. Keep this screen visible — the code is time-sensitive.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Visit the URL on your phone or computer">
        On your smartphone, tablet, or computer, open a browser and navigate to the URL shown on the TV. Sign in to your streaming account if prompted.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter the code">
        Type the code displayed on your Samsung TV into the field on your phone or computer and confirm.
      </Step>
      <Step title="TV refreshes and signs you in">
        Your Samsung TV will detect the completed activation and automatically refresh the app, signing you in. You're now ready to start streaming.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="LG">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Press the Home button">
        Press the **Home** button on your LG Magic Remote or standard LG remote to open the LG home screen launcher.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open the streaming app from LG Content Store">
        Navigate to the streaming app in your app row. If you haven't installed it yet, open the **LG Content Store**, search for the app, and install it before proceeding.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select Sign In">
        Launch the app and choose **Sign In**. If offered a choice, select the option that references signing in via a browser or another device to trigger the activation code flow.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Visit the URL displayed on a second device">
        On your phone, tablet, or computer, open a web browser and go to the URL shown on your LG TV's screen (e.g. `netflix.com/tv2`). Log in to your streaming account if you aren't already signed in.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter the code">
        In the activation field on your second device, carefully type the code displayed on your LG TV screen. Codes are usually 4–8 characters and are case-insensitive.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm and the TV signs in">
        Submit the code and wait a moment. Your LG TV will recognize the successful activation, and the streaming app will sign you in automatically.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Sony">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Press the Home button on the remote">
        Press the **Home** button on your Sony TV remote (Google TV or Android TV models) to open the main home screen.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Navigate to Apps">
        Scroll to the **Apps** row or section on the home screen. If you use a Google TV model, you may find apps pinned directly to the home screen.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open the streaming app">
        Select and open the streaming app you wish to use. If it is not yet installed, go to the **Google Play Store** on the TV to download and install it.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose Sign In">
        On the app's welcome screen, select **Sign In**. When prompted, choose the option to sign in using a web browser or another device to initiate the activation code process.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Note the activation URL and code">
        The Sony TV screen will display an activation URL and a unique code. Take note of both — or keep the TV visible from wherever you'll be using your second device.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sign in and enter the code on another device">
        On your phone, tablet, or computer, navigate to the activation URL in a browser. Sign in to your streaming account, then enter the code shown on your Sony TV.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Activation completes">
        Once the code is accepted, your Sony TV will refresh and display your streaming account's home screen, confirming that sign-in was successful.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Vizio">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Press V or Home on the remote">
        Press the **V** button (older Vizio remotes) or the **Home** button (newer SmartCast remotes) to open the Vizio SmartCast home screen.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open the streaming app">
        From the SmartCast home screen, locate the streaming app you want. If it is not visible, select **Apps** or the **WatchFree\+** tab and search for the app. Install it if needed.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select Sign In">
        Open the app and select **Sign In** on the welcome screen. Choose the sign-in method that uses a separate device or browser when offered.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Follow on-screen instructions to get the code">
        The TV will display a URL and an activation code. Make a note of them. Some Vizio apps also let you scan a QR code with your phone instead of typing the URL manually.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Visit the URL on your phone or computer">
        On a phone, tablet, or computer, open the URL shown on your Vizio TV and sign in to your streaming account.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter the code">
        Enter the activation code from your TV screen into the field on your second device and submit it.
      </Step>
      <Step title="TV signs you in">
        Your Vizio TV detects the successful activation and signs you in to the streaming app automatically. The app will load your account's home screen.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

## Tips for Smart TVs

<Tip>
  If the streaming app doesn't show an activation code option when you first open it, look for alternative sign-in labels such as **"Sign in on a web browser"**, **"Use another device to sign in"**, or **"Sign in with an activation code"**. Selecting one of these will start the code-based activation flow. Additionally, make sure your streaming app is fully up to date — manufacturers and streaming services frequently release updates that improve the sign-in experience. You can check for app updates in your TV's app store or settings menu.
</Tip>
