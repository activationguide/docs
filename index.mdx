Welcome to the **Streaming Help Center** — your go-to resource for signing in to streaming services on your TV or streaming device. Instead of typing your email and password with a remote control, most streaming platforms let you sign in using an **activation code**: a short code displayed on your TV screen that you enter on your phone or computer by visiting a URL like `netflix.com/tv2`. This Help Center walks you through every step of that process, covers all major device types, and helps you resolve any issues that come up along the way.

## Get Signed In — Quick Start

New to the activation code method? Follow these four steps and you'll be watching in minutes:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your streaming app on your TV">
    Turn on your TV or streaming device and launch the streaming app you want to sign in to — such as Netflix, Hulu, Disney+, or Max. If you haven't installed the app yet, find it in your device's app store and install it first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Sign In to get an activation code">
    On the app's welcome screen, choose **Sign In** (not "Sign Up"). The app will display a unique activation code — usually 6–8 characters — along with a short URL to visit on another device. Keep this screen open; the code is only valid for a limited time, typically 10–15 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="On your phone or computer, visit the URL shown on screen and enter the code">
    Open a browser on your smartphone, tablet, or computer and navigate to the URL displayed on your TV (for example, `netflix.com/tv2`). Sign in to your streaming account if prompted, then carefully type the activation code exactly as it appears on your TV screen — including any capital letters or numbers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your TV signs in automatically — start watching">
    Once you submit the code, your TV app detects the confirmation within seconds and signs you in automatically. No need to touch the remote again. Your profile, watchlist, and preferences will all load, and you're ready to start streaming.
  </Step>
</Steps>
