TV activation is a two-device sign-in method designed to make logging in to a streaming service on your TV as smooth as possible. The core idea is straightforward: your TV generates a temporary, unique code and displays it on screen. You then visit a short URL on your phone, tablet, or computer, log in with your streaming account credentials on that familiar device, and enter the code. The streaming service's servers see that the code matches the TV waiting to be signed in, confirm your identity, and silently push the sign-in to your TV — all without you ever having to hunt-and-peck a password using a remote control. The whole process typically takes under two minutes and is the same regardless of which streaming service or TV brand you are using.

## Step-by-Step Activation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Streaming App">
    Turn on your TV and launch the streaming app you want to sign in to — for example, Netflix. Navigate to the app using your remote and open it. If you are not already signed in, the app will display a **Sign In** screen rather than taking you directly to the content library. Look for a button or option that says **Sign In** or **Activate**, and select it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive Your Activation Code">
    After you select Sign In, the app will contact the streaming service's servers and generate a **unique activation code** just for your session. This code is typically 6–8 characters long — a mix of letters and numbers — and will be displayed prominently on your TV screen. The screen will also show the **URL you need to visit** on your other device, such as `netflix.com/tv2`. Keep your TV on this screen; do not navigate away or turn the TV off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Visit the URL on Another Device">
    Pick up your phone, tablet, or computer and open your preferred web browser. Type the URL shown on your TV screen into the address bar and press Go or Enter. Make sure to enter the URL exactly as shown — the page will load a sign-in and activation form hosted by the streaming service.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign In to Your Account">
    On the page that loads, you will be prompted to **log in to your streaming account**. Enter the email address and password associated with your account. If you have two-factor authentication enabled, complete that verification step as well. Once you are logged in, the page will typically advance automatically to the code-entry screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the Activation Code">
    You will see a text field asking for the activation code displayed on your TV. **Type or paste the code exactly as it appears on your TV screen.** Codes are case-sensitive, so pay attention to uppercase and lowercase letters. Double-check the code before submitting — a single incorrect character will cause the activation to fail and you will need to try again with a fresh code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="TV Signs In Automatically">
    After you submit the code, the streaming service verifies it against the session waiting on your TV. Within a few seconds — sometimes almost instantly — your **TV app will refresh and display your account's home screen**, ready to browse and watch. Your TV is now fully signed in. You can set down your phone and enjoy your content.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Why Codes Expire

<Warning>
  Activation codes are **single-use and time-limited**. Most streaming services set codes to expire after approximately **10–15 minutes** from the moment they are generated. If you take too long on any step — or if you navigate away from the TV's activation screen and return to it — your TV may display a brand-new code. The moment a new code appears, **the previous code is completely invalid** and will not work even if it was entered correctly. Always use the code currently shown on your TV screen, and if you see the code change, restart the process on your phone using the new code.
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting Tip

<Tip>
  If your TV does not automatically refresh and sign you in within about 30 seconds of successfully entering the code on your phone or computer, don't worry — this occasionally happens due to a minor network delay. Try pressing the **back button** on your TV remote and then re-opening the app, or use your remote to navigate to the app's home screen. If the TV is still stuck on the activation screen, fully **close and restart the app**. The app will check with the server on launch and should recognise that your account has already been activated.
</Tip>
